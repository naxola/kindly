## Which cookies we use

This site **does not use advertising or tracking cookies**. Analytics, if enabled, does not use cookies.

The {{productName}} application ({{appUrl}}) only uses **technical cookies** it needs to work:

| Cookie              | Purpose                                   | Duration |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Session             | Keep you signed in                        | 7 days   |
| Active organization | Remember which organization you work with | 1 year   |
| Language            | Remember the interface language           | 1 year   |

The theme (light or dark) is kept in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie, and never leaves it.

Technical cookies do not need consent. If others are added, this page and the notice will be updated first.
