How to ask us to delete your account and the associated data, what exactly is deleted and how long it takes.

## If you have an account on {{productName}}

Write to us from your account's email address at {{privacyEmail}} saying you want to delete your account. We will verify that the request comes from you and confirm when it is done.

If you are an administrator of an organization, also tell us whether you want to delete only your user or the whole organization with its contacts and conversations. We do not delete a whole organization without express confirmation: it affects the work of everyone else in it.

## If you are a member or client of an organization that uses {{productName}}

Your data is managed by that organization, not by us: it decides what is kept and for how long. To exercise your rights, contact it directly. If you write to us, we will forward your request and tell you who to continue with.

## What is deleted

- Your user, with its name, email and password, and your member profile.
- Your membership of the organization and the related invitations.
- If the organization is deleted: its contacts, affiliations, conversations, messages, tasks, knowledge base, procedures and activity history.
- The connections to messaging channels and the references to their credentials.

## What may remain, and why

- **Billing data**, for the period required by tax law.
- **Backups**, which are overwritten in their usual cycles. During that time they are isolated and not used for anything else.

## Timeframes

We acknowledge receipt within 72 hours at most and complete the deletion within 30 days at most from when we verify your identity.

## Disconnecting WhatsApp deletes nothing

Disconnecting your number from {{productName}} stops the sync but does not delete what was already synced. If you also want it deleted, ask for it expressly through the procedure above.

You can read the detail of what data we handle in the [privacy policy](/en/legal/privacy).
